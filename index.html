About

A Kolhapur bakery & cafe

Deluxe Bakery & Cafe serves cakes, pastries, snacks and beverages from Rajarampuri 4th Lane, and describes itself as a most-searched bakery on Zomato & Swiggy. Custom-themed celebration cakes are a speciality.
